Into the Mind

Below, is my Audio Project I've worked on

My audio project is an immersive audio project that explores the relentless nature of anxiety and finding your way into fantasy as a mindscape. Through haunting audio samples and dark ambient music, the piece captures the persistent, unsettling presence of thunder, breathing and steps as they echo through one’s mind. The layered soundscapes create a sense of tension and unease, visually sensing how anxiety can overshadow moments of peace. Ultimately, it leads to the fantasy of finding your final destination.

The software I used to work on my audio was Audacity. Audacity worked well with my project as I learned to navigate sounds, speed, and pitch. Along with ways to play around with sound and warp it. I also used Opengameart.org to sample my sounds and Youtube to as my main audio for the Dark Ambient background.
